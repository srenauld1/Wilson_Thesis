Barrier reanalysis: explicit control periods and measured-command bins

Open control_and_heat_bins within each barrier recording. Four eight-column figures retain the existing fluorescence-peak, slowdown, rotation-peak, and speed-up selections. Only the protocol classification is changed. The control figure uses control_trial==1 during placed-barrier intervals. Accommodation, unplaced-barrier lead-in, missing protocol labels, and full windows crossing trial/control/geometry boundaries are excluded. The saved fluorescence traces and event-detector thresholds are unchanged.

Heat-trial events (control_trial==0) are binned by median measured heat command over -0.5:+0.5 seconds around the event: 0 to <1 V, 1 to <2.5 V, and >=2.5 V. Median command below -0.05 V is excluded; small negative monitor noise is assigned to the low bin. A zero-voltage interval in a heat trial remains in the low-heat bin. These bins describe command voltage, not measured temperature. Shared axes/color limits across the four figures permit visual comparison. Empty bins are displayed explicitly.

Newer records use aligned protocol trial_number, control_trial, barrier_active, and geometry. Older records use aligned Virmen control_trial and currTt; only types 1/2 with a finite nonsentinel barrier distance are active. Accommodation type 3 and distance sentinel 100000 are excluded. Heat is taken from the measured DAQ channel (heatDAQ in legacy Virmen tables, dq(1).heat in native files), rather than the legacy virtual heat profile.

April 27 trial 5 has no saved Virmen table. Its labels and positions are recovered from raw vr_test_1 data using the recorded 60-Hz DAQ iteration clock and the existing pipeline's end-offset convention. Only raw trial types 1/2 after the documented five-second placement delay are active. Its barrier-placement boundaries are reconstructed, unlike the explicitly logged modern barrier_active flag. The final custom state write can extend one sample beyond position/time; no unmatched sample is used. This source and active definition are saved in settings.

flat_paths_dff_page_*.png/.fig show the active portion of each individual barrier trial. Panels identify control versus heat and their time range. Gaps remain missing; scatter plots do not connect across them. All finite valid samples are retained, with color limits at the recording's 5th/95th dF/F percentiles shared across panels. High dF/F points are drawn last. Green marks the first plotted point. A dashed barrier is drawn where its position/normal were explicitly logged. Legacy paths retain saved virtual-arena coordinates; their barrier line is not reconstructed.

events.csv includes every original event-group entry and its control flag, barrier trial, median command, bin, or exclusion reason. Events repeated in the all-slowdown and turn-subgroup columns are separate group entries, not additional unique events. binned_analysis.mat stores condition arrays, classifications, aligned protocol/position signals, and settings. flat_path_trials.csv indexes the path panels. control_heat_bins_manifest.csv summarizes recording completion.

The earlier broad heat-on/off figures are retained under previous_voltage_on_off. April 24 trial 2 remains awaiting ROI extraction. Other geometry folders are unchanged.
