Validation completed 2026-11-04.

The original inhibition Python functions window_features() and bouts() from
Behavior_analysis/analyze_ves041_inhibition_pivot_frequency.py were run directly
on source recorded heading and cached matching XY coordinates in all ten
assessable no-heat recordings. All 243 detected bouts matched the MATLAB
implementation: merged boundaries, representative-window sample indices, net
rotation, net displacement, and directionality (feature tolerance 1e-6).

Of those 243 detections, 238 had complete valid -2 to +3 second plotting context.
The figure uses those 238 epochs, with each mapped fly weighted equally.
No heat-cohort recordings were analyzed.
